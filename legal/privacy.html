Privacy Policy

Version 2.1 — Effective 12 November 2026

Preamble

This policy informs users of the Unydex application and its associated website (together, "the App") about the processing of personal data carried out when they use them, in accordance with Articles 12 to 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of 27 April 2016 (the "GDPR"), the French Data Protection Act No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 as amended, and its Article 82 on trackers.

It forms an integral part of the Terms of Use. The French version prevails in case of discrepancy.

Key points.

  • Without an account, your collection stays on your device. Caught Pokémon, boxes, teams, cards, binders and preferences are stored in the App's local storage and sent to no one.
  • Some features query third-party services, even without an account: the card catalogue and images, prices, the exchange rate. Those services receive your device's IP address and the request (Section 3).
  • An account is optional. It lets you restore your collection on another device and sync it in real time (Section 4).
  • The App Store version of the App displays ads served by Google, personalised only with your consent (Section 5). The Unydex+ subscription removes them.
  • The PokéAdvisor assistant sends nothing until you save your own Mistral AI key; it then sends your questions and a summary of your collection to Mistral AI (Section 6).
  • The camera sends nothing: recognition runs on the device.
  • No data is sold, rented or transferred.

2 — Data stored on your device

The App stores locally, on your device:

  • your collection: caught Pokémon (regular and shiny), box contents, order, names and wallpapers, battle teams, owned cards and their copies (version, condition, date added), favourites, wishlist and its target prices, binders, price and collection value history, a log of recent actions;
  • the number of cards scanned today (free version limit);
  • your preferences: theme, language, game version, display options, scan mode, sounds and haptics, animations, price source and currency, the development build's ad setting;
  • PokéAdvisor settings, memory, conversation and your Mistral AI access key;
  • a cache of downloaded catalogue data (Section 3), for offline use.

This information is sent to no one, except as described in Sections 3 to 6. It is erased when the App is uninstalled. Your Mistral AI key is never sent to the controller, nor included in backups or sync.

Legal basis for local storage: it is strictly necessary for the service you request and therefore exempt from consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act).

2.1. Camera recognition

  • Images are processed entirely on the device, by machine-learning models built into the App.
  • No image is transmitted or stored: frames are analysed and immediately discarded.
  • Camera access is requested by the operating system and can be refused or revoked at any time in the device settings, without affecting the rest of the App.

2.2. File backup

You can export all your data to a file and import it. The file is handed over through the system share sheet; what happens to it is your choice. Your Mistral AI key is not included.

4 — Data processed if you create an account

4.1. Categories of data

  • Identification — email address; password, stored as a hash and never readable; internal identifier; where applicable, the name provided by your identity provider.
  • Profile — username; profile picture, which is a number pointing to an illustration within the App, not a file; creation and update dates.
  • Collection — all data described in Section 2, except the Mistral AI key and device-specific display preferences. It therefore includes PokéAdvisor's memory and conversation, and the game version chosen for the free team.
  • Technical data — date of last update; type of originating device ("iPhone" or "Browser"), with no device identifier or name.

4.2. Sign-in with an identity provider

When you sign in with Google or Apple, the provider shares your email address and, where applicable, your name, and nothing else. Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email address. The provider's processing is governed by its own privacy policy. When an account created with Apple is deleted, the App asks Apple for a fresh confirmation and sends the resulting code to Apple, through the Service's server, to revoke the App's access; nothing is kept.

4.3. Sync

Your collection is stored on the hosting provider's servers and synced in real time across devices signed in to the same account: a persistent connection to the hosting provider is kept open while the App is open. When the same data has been changed on two devices, both versions are merged automatically.

4.4. Account shared with Cartodex

The account is shared between Unydex and the Cartodex app, by the same publisher. The first time an account signs in to Unydex, its Cartodex backup, if any, is read and added to the collection. Deleting the account erases the data of both apps.

4.5. Shared favourites counter

A card's page may display the number of other accounts that have favourited it. It is computed by the hosting provider from stored collections, shown only to signed-in users, and never reveals the identity, username or collection of any account.

4.6. Data not collected

The controller does not collect: location, contacts, calendar, photo library, microphone, health data, browsing history, installed apps or payment data. Subscription payments are handled by Apple (Section 7).

4.7. No profiling by the controller

The controller carries out no profiling within the meaning of Article 4(4) GDPR and no decision based solely on automated processing within the meaning of Article 22. Account data is never used for marketing.

4.8. Friends and messaging

Each account receives a six-digit friend code, assigned at random when the account is created and never changed. A user who shares it allows another user to send a friend request, which they are free to accept or decline.

  • Data processed: the friend code; requests and friendships (accounts concerned, dates); messages exchanged between friends (content, time sent and read); blocks; reports (the reported message, copied, and its reason); friend-code attempts (time only, to limit attempts to ten per hour).
  • What a friend sees: nickname, avatar and friend code; the Pokédex (caught Pokémon); the cards in the collection, without their condition, dates or prices; the teams. No other account data is available to them (conversations with others, preferences, activity log, email address).
  • Purpose and legal basis: providing the social feature requested by the user — performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)). Moderation and abuse prevention: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Moderation: links are refused and the most common insults are masked automatically; any message received can be reported from the conversation and any user blocked. Reports are reviewed by the controller, who may delete an account that breaches the terms of use.
  • Confidentiality of messages: they are encrypted in transit and stored by the host (European Union), but are not end-to-end encrypted: the controller is technically able to access them. It only does so following a report or a request from an authority.
  • Retention: messages are deleted when either user removes or blocks the other, and when either account is deleted. Reports are kept for at most one year after they are handled; friend-code attempts for one day.

6 — PokéAdvisor (chat assistant)

This feature is not offered in the current version of the App: no data is sent to Mistral AI. This section will only apply once it is made available.

PokéAdvisor is an assistant based on Mistral AI language models (Mistral AI SAS, 15 rue des Halles, 75001 Paris, France). It only works with an access key that you obtain yourself from Mistral AI and save in the App; you are then a Mistral AI customer, under its terms.

  • Sent to Mistral AI with each question: the question, the last twelve messages of the conversation, and a summary of your collection — Living Dex progress, number and value of cards, the fifteen most valuable cards, binders, items memorised by the assistant. The App tells you this before you save your key.
  • The data goes directly from your device to Mistral AI; the controller has no access to it. Mistral AI processes it under its own privacy policy (mistral.ai/terms).
  • The conversation and the assistant's memory are stored on the device and, for signed-in users, in the account (Section 4.1).
  • Do not share personal information with the assistant (name, address, contact details, sensitive data): it would be sent to Mistral AI and kept in the conversation.
  • Legal basis: performance of the service you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
